# Ethics and limitations

LearnBridge is an educational prototype, not a substitute for teachers, academic advisors, or professional training guidance. Its resources, learner profiles, and relevance judgments are synthetic. Rule-generated judgments can favor the same assumptions embedded in generation. Metadata quality and accessibility are simulated rather than audited. Provider exposure control improves list balance but does not establish provider fairness, content correctness, learner outcomes, or equal access. English is the only generated language. Resource URLs are illustrative.

The explanation layer may only repeat dataset metadata. It must not invent certificates, instructor credentials, ratings, accreditation, employment outcomes, salaries, or guarantees. Any future use with real learners requires consent, privacy safeguards, subgroup evaluation, accessibility review, educator oversight, and monitoring for disparate outcomes.
