# Five-minute pitch outline

1. **0:00–0:40 — Problem:** click-optimized learning feeds can ignore time, budget, accessibility, gaps, and exposure.
2. **0:40–1:20 — Product:** onboard a cold-start learner and demonstrate the same profile across three systems.
3. **1:20–2:20 — Method:** popularity baseline, TF-IDF retrieval, then hard constraints plus hybrid scoring and MMR.
4. **2:20–3:20 — Evidence:** show computed model comparison, constraint satisfaction, catalog coverage, and lambda trade-off figures.
5. **3:20–4:15 — Language adaptation:** SmolLM2-135M LoRA learned caption rejection (0% to 75%) but regressed overall accuracy (25% to 18.75%) and valid-label rate (100% to 43.75%). Explain why deterministic filters remain authoritative.
6. **4:15–5:00 — Responsibility and close:** synthetic-data limits, provider cap, auditable rejection reasons, and next steps with real educator-reviewed judgments.
